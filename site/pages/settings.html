s opens the settings. Every change takes effect at once and is saved.

KeyAction
s / Escclose

The settings are kept in settings.toml in the platform config directory and may be edited by hand. The About page, linked from the settings, shows the version, the license and which native libraries were found where.

Default apps

Under "Default apps" the settings make pixpixpix the default app for a file type, or for all images or all videos at once, and give the type back to the app it replaced, which they remember. pixpixpix becomes a default only there, never by being installed. While it is the default, o opens files in the app it replaced.

On Linux this goes through xdg-mime, on macOS through Launch Services. The Flatpak can only show the defaults, not change them.
