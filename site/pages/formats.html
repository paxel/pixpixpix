Native libraries

pixpixpix itself is a plain Rust program. A few formats and features need system libraries, which are loaded only the first time they are needed, never at start, so they never slow down the first image. Without them everything else works; the feature that needs them says what is missing. The About page, linked from the settings (s), lists which of them were found and where.

libheif decodes through plugins: HEIC needs its libde265 plugin and AVIF its dav1d or aom plugin. Some distributions package these separately. The Flatpak and the macOS app bring what they need; the packages per system:

Phone videos on Fedora

Fedora ships ffmpeg-free, which cannot decode H.264 and HEVC, the codecs of most phone videos; such a video then says its codec cannot be decoded. The full ffmpeg from RPM Fusion decodes them:

sudo dnf swap ffmpeg-free ffmpeg --allowerasing
