A fast, keyboard-first image and video viewer for Linux and macOS.

pixpixpix photo.jpg (or the short pix3 photo.jpg) puts the picture on screen as fast as possible; nothing else, not even the folder scan, runs before the first image is shown. Started on a folder or without an argument, it shows the folder's first image. From there you step through the folder with the arrow keys, while the neighbours are preloaded so the next image is already there.

Find your way

  • Install it with Homebrew, as a Flatpak or from a release.
  • Getting around: step through files, zoom, play videos, and move between the image view, the band, the dir column and the overview.
  • Turning and editing: turn, annotate, copy and open elsewhere.
  • Trash and undo: trash files, put them back, browse every trash.
  • Settings and default apps.
  • Formats and libraries and Reporting a problem.

What it does

  • Browse. Left and Right step through the folder in natural name order (img2 before img10), with a band of the preloaded neighbours. Files other programs add, remove or rename show up on their own.
  • Move through folders. A dir column lists the current folder among its siblings; moving through it moves the image view along.
  • Overview. A scrollable gallery of the current folder and every folder below it, with thumbnails shared with other apps through the freedesktop thumbnail cache.
  • Act on the file. Rotate (JPEG losslessly), move to the trash and back, copy to the clipboard, or open in another app.
  • Videos and moving photos. Videos play through libmpv with seeking and frame stepping; Apple Live Photos and Google/Samsung Motion Photos play their moment. Animated GIF and WebP play on their own.
  • Annotate and export. Arrows, text, outlines, pen, highlighter, pixelation and a free crop, plus an export of an area at an exact pixel size, all behind one save dialog that never overwrites by accident.
  • Metadata. A panel with camera, lens, exposure, capture date and location; the capture date can be corrected in place, and the location or all EXIF can be removed.
