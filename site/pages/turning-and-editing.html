Turn a picture and write the turn into the file, draw on it and save a copy, or hand it to another app.

Turning

In the image view a turn shows on screen first and is written with Enter; in the overview it is written at once. JPEG is turned losslessly through libturbojpeg (see Formats and libraries). HEIC/HEIF, AVIF and files with several images are not turned, as the other images would be lost; videos cannot be turned.

The annotation toolbar

p opens the tools: arrows, text, outlines, pen, highlighter, pixelation and a free crop, plus an export of an area at an exact pixel size, all behind one save dialog that never overwrites by accident. Paused video frames can be annotated and saved as images too.

KeyAction

Entersave (opens the dialog)

In the save dialog, Enter saves as a new file and Esc goes back to editing.

Copy and open elsewhere

KeyAction
ccopy the picture as shown, for chats and image apps
Shift+Ccopy the file, for file managers
oopen in another app

In the overview c and Shift+C copy the selected file's picture and file. Which app o opens is chosen in the settings.

Metadata

i opens a panel with camera, lens, exposure, capture date and location. The capture date can be corrected in place, and the location or all EXIF can be removed.
